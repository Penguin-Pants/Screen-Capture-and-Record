📋 About This Project

ScreenCapture Pro is a powerful, feature-rich screenshot and screen recording extension built with modern web technologies. It's designed to be intuitive, fast, and completely free for everyone.

All captures are processed locally on your device - no data is ever sent to external servers. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.
